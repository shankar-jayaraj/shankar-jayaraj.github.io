Booking FAQs.

What formats do you offer?

Keynotes (45–60 min), invited talks, half-day to multi-day FDPs, hands-on workshops and masterclasses, in person across India or online worldwide.

What do you charge, and how does pricing work?

There is no flat rate, because a 45-minute online keynote and a two-day residential FDP are different pieces of work. Four things move the number:

  • Format — keynote, workshop or multi-day FDP
  • Duration — a single session or a programme across several days
  • Audience size and level — a 60-person faculty cohort differs from a 500-seat auditorium
  • Travel — online, in-state, or elsewhere in India
